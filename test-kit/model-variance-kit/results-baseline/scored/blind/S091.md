# S091

## Original

You can create maintenance plans to suppress alerts during planned work. You'll see the plan appear in the list once you save it, and you can stop it early if you need to. You need Admin permissions to create or edit a maintenance plan. You may want to notify your team before you start a plan.


## Rewrite

# Maintenance Plans

Maintenance plans suppress alerts during planned work.

## Permissions

Admin permissions are required to create or edit a maintenance plan.

## Creating a Plan

After you save a maintenance plan, it appears in the maintenance plans list.

## Stopping a Plan

You can stop an active maintenance plan before its scheduled end time.

## Recommended Practice

Notify your team before starting a maintenance plan.
